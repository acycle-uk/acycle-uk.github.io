KIT — understanding property price dynamics

KIT is a research project examining the evolution of UK residential property prices, including patterns of relative price movement across price groups. The research paper, analytical software and supporting data will be made available here as they are released.

About the project

KIT develops a repeat-sales representation of UK residential property prices and examines the organisation of price changes across groups and through time.

The planned public materials distinguish the research paper, the code used for analysis and the input datasets needed to reproduce specified results.
